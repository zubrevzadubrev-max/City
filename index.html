Tashkent City Mall

Tashkent City Mall is one of the most modern shopping and entertainment places in Tashkent. It combines shopping, food, entertainment and comfort in one modern location.

What can you buy? Why is it better?
CITY MALL

About the mall

Tashkent City Mall is different from a traditional bazaar because everything is located in one modern and comfortable place. Visitors can shop, eat, relax and spend time with friends and family.

What can you buy?

You can find many different types of products in the mall.

👕

Clothes and shoes

You can buy fashionable clothes, jackets, sneakers, sportswear and shoes.

📱

Electronics

Smartphones, headphones, gadgets and accessories are also available.

💄

Beauty products

You can find perfumes, cosmetics and skincare products.

🎁

Gifts

Visitors can choose souvenirs, watches, bags and accessories.

🍔

Food

There are cafés, restaurants and places with local and international food.

🏠

Home products

You can also find useful products for your home and lifestyle.

Why is it better than a bazaar?

Tashkent City Mall

  • Comfortable in any weather
  • Many shops in one place
  • Modern cafés and restaurants
  • Clean and stylish atmosphere
  • Shopping and entertainment together

Traditional Bazaar

  • Traditional atmosphere
  • Fresh products
  • You can bargain
  • Can be crowded
  • Less comfortable in bad weather

My opinion

In my opinion, Tashkent City Mall is a better place for a modern shopping day. It is stylish, organized and comfortable. You can buy many different things and also relax with friends or family.
